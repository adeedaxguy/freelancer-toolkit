Ahrefs content gap research is useful only when it turns into a better page. A list of keywords is not a strategy. The brief needs to explain which query matters, what competitors answer, what they miss, and how your page will be more useful.

Use the [Ahrefs Content Gap Brief Generator](https://freeltools.com/tools/ahrefs-content-gap-brief-generator) after reviewing competitor URLs in Ahrefs, Ahrefs Bar, Google SERPs, and related searches.

## Quick answer

An Ahrefs content gap brief should include the target keyword, competitor URL, page type, missing angle, related searches, internal links, conversion path, schema needs, and a short answer section for AEO and GEO visibility.

The goal is not to copy the competitor. The goal is to understand why their page ranks and then build a cleaner, more useful page around the searcher's actual job.

## What to collect before generating the brief

Capture these fields from Ahrefs and the live SERP:

- Primary keyword and country.
- Competing URL and page type.
- Keyword gap or subtopic gap.
- SERP features such as People also ask, image pack, video, or AI overview.
- Related searches at the bottom of Google.
- Backlink or authority gap if the competitor is much stronger.
- Your outperformance angle.

For free-tool SEO, the outperformance angle is usually practical utility: a tool that works immediately, a clearer explanation, screenshots or examples, and internal links to the next step.

## How to judge the competitor page type

Do not compare every result the same way. A tool page, affiliate roundup, glossary article, SaaS landing page, calculator, template, and forum thread all win for different reasons. Ahrefs can show the keywords and backlinks, but the page type explains the searcher's expectation.

If the competitor is a tool page, the gap may be speed, privacy, exact inputs, or clearer examples. If the competitor is a blog post, the gap may be missing screenshots, outdated examples, weak internal links, or no downloadable template. If the competitor is a high-authority roundup, the gap may be harder to close with content alone, so the better move is to create a more useful original asset and promote it.

## What the brief should produce

A good brief should be specific enough that a writer, SEO, or developer can build the page without guessing. Include:

- The exact page to create or refresh.
- The primary keyword and secondary related searches.
- The competitor URLs reviewed.
- The missing sections to add.
- The tool or template that makes the page more useful.
- The internal links that should point to the page.
- The conversion path after the visitor finishes the task.
- The schema type that fits the page.

For FreelTools, that often means routing a blog post into a working tool page, then into another calculator, checklist, or generator that keeps the visitor solving the same problem.

## When the gap is a backlink gap

If the competitor wins mostly because of authority, do not publish a thin imitation page. Create a useful original asset, statistic, template, calculator, or comparison that gives editors a reason to reference you.

## Example content gap brief

If Ahrefs shows that competitors rank for "on page SEO audit tool" and related searches like "SEO audit checklist" and "GSC page refresh," the brief should not simply say "write an SEO audit article." It should say:

- Refresh the on-page audit tool page.
- Add a short answer explaining what the audit checks.
- Add a GSC low-CTR workflow for pages that already get impressions.
- Link to title, meta description, robots, internal-link, and schema tools.
- Add a supporting blog post for the refresh workflow.
- Add FAQ schema around pasted HTML, live URL limitations, indexing requests, and score interpretation.

That turns keyword research into a page that can compete on usefulness, not just keyword repetition.
